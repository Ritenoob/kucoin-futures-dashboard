Dashboard Improvement
Add Modifiable Price Threshold Column to Dashboard
User Clive Tyler brought up a good idea (March 13, 2021) of adding modifiable price thresholds to inputs, and I thought, even better, let's add a modifiable price column threshold to dashboard.



This would act as a supplemental to the pip threshold we already have. User can still indicate pip threshold for trailing stop activation, such as 50 pips, and then when the trades enter dashboard with their 50 pip threshold, user can see these thresholds at their price level, and furthermore, he can manually modify these thresholds up or down.  

Use Case: Perhaps user has entered 50 pip threshold for all his 10+ orders, but when they get entered and controlled in UTS dashboard, he wants to manually change some of them, up or down in pips. With this price threshold in dashboard, he can do so.
